Referral System


At DefiNexus, we believe in rewarding our community for helping us grow. Our referral system allows users to earn rewards by inviting friends and expanding the DeFi ecosystem.

How It Works:

1. Get Your Referral Link

Each registered user receives a unique referral link that you can share across your networks.

2. Invite Friends

Share your link with friends, family, or your community through social media, messaging apps, or email.

3. Earn Rewards

When your referrals join and interact with DefiNexus activities, you receive a percentage of their eligible transactions as a bonus.

Why Join the Referral Program?


  • Passive Income – Earn without extra effort as your network grows and engages with the platform.
  • Unlimited Earnings – No cap on referrals means your earning potential is limitless.
  • Community Growth – Help strengthen the DeFi ecosystem while being rewarded for your contribution.

Referral Rewards Structure


Level Reward Rate Activities
Direct Referral (Level 1) 5% All eligible transactions and mining rewards
Secondary Referral (Level 2) 2% All eligible transactions and mining rewards
Tertiary Referral (Level 3) 1% All eligible transactions and mining rewards

Tracking Your Referrals


Our intuitive dashboard allows you to track all your referrals in real-time. Monitor:


  • Total number of referrals
  • Active vs. inactive referrals
  • Rewards earned from each referral
  • Historical data and performance statistics
