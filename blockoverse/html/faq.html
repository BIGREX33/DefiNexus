How does the yield optimization work?

Our yield optimization system uses advanced algorithms to:

  1. Monitor yield rates across various DeFi platforms in real-time
  2. Automatically reallocate assets to the highest yielding protocols
  3. Compound returns periodically to maximize growth
  4. Minimize transaction costs by batching operations

This dynamic rebalancing ensures your assets are always generating optimal returns without requiring manual monitoring or adjustments.

What are DefiNexus governance tokens?

The DNX token is our native governance token that gives holders voting rights on protocol decisions and access to premium features. DNX token utilities include:

  • Voting on protocol upgrades and parameter changes
  • Staking for additional yield and platform rewards
  • Fee discounts on platform transactions
  • Access to premium strategies and features

DNX token holders also receive a portion of platform fees distributed proportionally to their staked amount.

Rewards & Referrals

How does the referral system work?

Our multi-level referral system rewards you for growing the DefiNexus community:

  1. Each user receives a unique referral link to share
  2. When someone joins through your link, they become your direct referral
  3. You earn rewards based on your referrals' activities:
    • Level 1 (direct referrals): 5% of eligible transactions and mining rewards
    • Level 2 (referrals of your referrals): 2% of eligible transactions and mining rewards
    • Level 3: 1% of eligible transactions and mining rewards

All rewards are automatically credited to your account and can be viewed in your referral dashboard.

When and how are referral rewards paid out?

Referral rewards are processed in real-time and credited to your account immediately after your referrals complete eligible transactions. There's no minimum threshold to accumulate before receiving rewards.

Your earned rewards are visible in your referral dashboard and can be:

  • Withdrawn to your wallet
  • Reinvested into DeFi strategies
  • Converted to DNX tokens with a 5% bonus

All earnings are paid in the original transaction currency or can be automatically converted to a stablecoin of your choice.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No, there is absolutely no limit to how many people you can refer to DefiNexus. The more active users you bring to the platform, the more rewards you can earn.

Our three-tier referral structure means you benefit not only from direct referrals but also when those users refer others, creating a potential exponential growth in your passive income stream.

Getting Started

How do I create an account on DefiNexus?

Getting started with DefiNexus is simple:

  1. Visit our website and click on "Connect Wallet"
  2. Connect your preferred Web3 wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.)
  3. Sign a message to verify wallet ownership (no transaction fee)
  4. Complete your profile with basic information (optional)
  5. Set up 2FA for additional security (recommended)

There are no KYC requirements for basic platform usage, maintaining your privacy while using DefiNexus.

What wallets are compatible with DefiNexus?

DefiNexus supports all major Web3 wallets including:

  • MetaMask
  • WalletConnect
  • Coinbase Wallet
  • Trust Wallet
  • Ledger (via MetaMask or WalletConnect)
  • Trezor (via MetaMask or WalletConnect)

For optimal experience, we recommend using wallets that support the Avalanche C-Chain natively.

Are there any fees for using DefiNexus?

DefiNexus operates with a transparent fee structure:

  • Protocol Fee: 0.3% on swaps and transactions
  • Performance Fee: 2% on profits from automated yield strategies
  • Network Fees: Standard blockchain gas fees apply (minimized through batching)

DNX token holders receive discounts on protocol fees based on their staking level:

  • Bronze (100+ DNX): 10% discount
  • Silver (500+ DNX): 25% discount
  • Gold (1000+ DNX): 50% discount
